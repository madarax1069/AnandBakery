Our Story

Three generations, one obsession with the perfect bake.

Anand Bakery began in 1987 as a small counter on Park Street, where Late Shri Ramesh Chandra began his mornings at four, shaping dough by hand. Today, his son and grandson carry forward the same recipes — the same butter, the same slow ferment, the same patience — quietly, one tray at a time.

We don't chase trends. We chase the smell of warm bread, the crackle of a fresh croissant, and the smile of a regular who's been ordering the same vanilla pastry for thirty-five years. That's the whole bakery.
